# Dominion playtest — k3 "Size Distortion (2nd)", 4 players

**Kingdom:** Artisan, Bandit, Bureaucrat, Chapel, Festival, Gardens, Sentry, Throne Room, Witch, Workshop (no landscapes/extras)

**Setup:** 4 players, 7 Copper + 3 Estate each. Supply: Province/Duchy/Estate/Gardens 12, Curse 30, Kingdom 10, Copper 32, Silver 40, Gold 30.
Shuffles via `node shuffle.cjs k3-4p-P<n>-<k>` (k = that player's shuffle count, 1 = opening shuffle). Turn order P1→P4.

**Players & plans**

- P1 Collector — Gardens rush: Workshops (up to 8) + Festivals for +Buy at $5, Artisan once at $6; switches to Gardens (buys and Workshop gains) from its 7th turn or at 6 Workshops; spare buys/$0-1 on Copper, $2-3 on Estate; Duchy/Estate when Gardens run out.
- P2 Pragmatist — Witch + Big Money: Silver/Silver on 4/3 (Witch on 5/2); up to 2 Witches; Gold at $6-7, Province at $8; Duchy at $5-7 when Provinces ≤4-5, Estate late.
- P3 Engine builder — Chapel opening, trashes Curses/Estates/Coppers (keeps ≥$6 total money), 1 Witch, Sentry ×2, Festivals (up to 5), Throne Rooms (up to 2), Gold; Provinces once the deck has economy.
- P4 Aggressor — attacks first: Witch ×2, Bandit ×2, Bureaucrat ×2, Festival only to fix terminal glut; Gold/Province otherwise.

Adaptation rules shared by P2–P4: once an opponent holds ≥4 Gardens, they deny Gardens at $4 (and at $5 instead of Silver). Nobody takes the last Province or the card that empties a 3rd pile when that would lose them the game. Victims choose: Bandit trashes Silver before Gold; Bureaucrat topdecks Estate before Gardens/Duchy/Province.

### Playtester notes

- Curse bookkeeping checks out: 30 Curses dealt (P1 10, P2 7, P3 7 of which 5 trashed, P4 6). Estates: 12 in supply, P1 took 10, P3 took 2. Gardens: P1 10, P3 1, P4 1 (the last two were denial buys).
- Nobody contested the Gardens until round 13. Three Witches handing out Curses fattened P1's deck for free: 10 of P1's 58 cards were Curses. Gardens went from 3 to 4 VP at T14 and to 5 VP at T17. Those three Gardens-threshold turns were the biggest VP swings of the game.
- P1 had 5 terminal collisions (Workshop stuck in hand) and had $0-2 on 17 of its 23 turns. The plan still won by about 17 VP. From T18 on P1 was locked out of anything useful and simply piled out on Estates.
- The engine never came together. Two Golds at $6/$7 and few $5 hands meant P3 bought no Festival and never bought a Throne Room either, so P3 played Chapel/Big Money. The Province race stalled with 1 left from T20 on: every money player declined the last Province because P1's lead made it a losing ending.
- The Aggressor only ever had Witch and Bureaucrat to attack with. Bandit was never bought (Witch and then Festival came first at $5). Bureaucrat was played 4 times; it hit all three opponents on 3 of them, and P3 blocked it once (no Victory card in hand). Witch was the only attack that decided anything, and it helped P1's Gardens.
- Fun read: a high-interaction and swingy middle game, then a long, flat endgame (5 rounds of Duchy/Estate shuffling with the last Province untouchable). The Workshop/Gardens rush is very strong in 4p with 3 Witch players feeding it.
