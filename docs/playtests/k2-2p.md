# Dominion playtest: k2 (Repetition, 2nd ed. recommended), 2 players

**Kingdom:** Festival, Harbinger, Militia, Workshop, Caravan, Explorer, Outpost, Pearl Diver, Pirate Ship, Treasury (no landscapes)

**Setup:** 7 Copper + 3 Estate each; 8 of each Victory card, 10 per Kingdom pile, 10 Curses. Shuffles came from `shuffle.cjs` with seeds `k2-2p-P<n>-<shuffleNumber>` (shuffle 1 = opening deck). Seed numbers are in the notes.

**Players**

- **P1 Comboist** builds around the Outpost + Treasury + Caravan combo. Treasuries go back on top of the deck at Clean-up (when no Victory card was bought), so the 3-card Outpost hand starts with them. Caravan pays out its next-turn card immediately in the extra turn. Pearl Diver adds a little topdecking. Gold at $6-7, Province at $8, Duchy late.
- **P2 Pragmatist** plays Militia Big Money: Militia, Silver at $3-5, Gold at $6-7, Province at $8, Duchy when 5 or fewer Provinces are left, Estate near the end.

Neither player bought Pirate Ship, Workshop, Explorer, Harbinger or Festival. Pirate Ship is weak against a 2-player money deck, and the other four were never the best buy at the price.

**Conventions:** `T8+` is the Outpost extra turn after T8. `hand` is the starting hand after any Caravan duration draw, so it includes cards Militia left after the discard. `played` lists actions and treasures in the order they were played, including cards drawn mid-turn. Treasury topdecks are always taken when allowed. Durations stay in play until the Clean-up of the turn they resolve on.

## Result

- **Winner: P1 Comboist, 40 VP to 33.** The game ended when P1 bought the last Province on T19.
- **Length:** 19 turns for P1 (plus 8 Outpost extra turns, 27 turns played) and 18 turns for P2. 45 turns in total.
- **Provinces:** P1 5 (T8+, T13, T15, T17+, T19), P2 3 (T11, T13, T16). **Duchies:** P1 2, P2 4. Duchy pile ended with 2 left.
- **P1 deck (37 cards):** 1 Pearl Diver, 7 Copper, 2 Gold, 4 Silver, 2 Outpost, 3 Caravan, 4 Estate, 4 Treasury, 5 Province, 2 Duchy, 1 Militia
- **P2 deck (28 cards):** 6 Silver, 7 Copper, 4 Gold, 3 Province, 3 Estate, 4 Duchy, 1 Militia
- **Supply at end:** Province 0, Duchy 2, Estate 7, Gold 24, Silver 30, Treasury 6, Caravan 7, Outpost 8, Militia 8, Pearl Diver 9. Every other pile was untouched.

**Assessment:** The Outpost, Treasury and Caravan synergy really works in this kingdom. P1 got 8 extra turns. The Treasury loop in T12+ and T13 and the Outpost pair in T17 decided the game.
P2 Militia Big Money kept pace until about T16 and never had a dead turn.
P1 had real frustration turns: T10, when Militia left a 3-card hand, and the Estate-clogged T16+ and T18+ extra turns.
Interaction was limited to Militia. Half the kingdom (Pirate Ship, Workshop, Explorer, Harbinger, Festival) went unused. The game was close and had swings, but it was narrow in strategy.
