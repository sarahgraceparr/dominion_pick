# Dominion playtest k1 — 2 players

**Kingdom (k1, "Peril combo"):** Squire, Market Square, Sage, Feodum, Fortress, Maroon, Rope, Tools, Cutthroat, Pendant
**Landscape:** Peril (Event, $2: you may trash an Action card from your hand to gain a Loot)
**Setup:** Shelters (Necropolis, Hovel, Overgrown Estate + 7 Copper). 2-player piles: 8 per Victory pile (Feodum 8), 10 per Kingdom pile, 46 Copper left in supply, 30-card Loot pile (2 of each of 15 Loots, shuffled with seed `k1-2p-loot-1`; not a Supply pile).
**Randomness:** every shuffle used `shuffle.cjs` with seed `k1-2p-<P1|P2>-<n>` (top card first). 12 shuffles total (incl. Loot pile).

**Players and plans**

- **P1 "Comboist":** the Fortress + Peril loop. Keep one Fortress in hand, then each Buy + $2 trashes it with Peril for a $7 Loot (Fortress comes back to hand). Hold Market Square in hand so each trash also gains a Gold. Get +Buys from Loots, Rope, Squire and Market Square, and trash Necropolis/Squire with Peril too. Start greening when the deck has about 4 Gold/Loot.
- **P2 "Pragmatist":** Big Money with Cutthroat (one, as the terminal), plus Pendant and Rope. Uses Peril on Necropolis when there's a spare $2. Buys Gold/Province, then Duchy/Estate at the end.

## Result

- **Winner:** P2 (Pragmatist), **37 – 21**
- **Game length:** 13 turns each (P2 took the last Province on T13). End condition: Provinces gone.
- **P1 deck (30 cards):** 7x Copper, 3x Gold, 3x Province, 2x Insignia, 2x Fortress, 2x Silver, 1x Prize Goat, 1x Market Square, 1x Jewels, 1x Rope, 1x Duchy, 1x Squire, 1x Maroon, 1x Amphora, 1x Orb, 1x Figurehead, 1x Endless Chalice
- **P2 deck (27 cards):** 7x Copper, 5x Province, 4x Silver, 2x Gold, 2x Duchy, 1x Pendant, 1x Rope, 1x Cutthroat, 1x Puzzle Box, 1x Estate, 1x Prize Goat, 1x Sword
- Loot pile left: 18/30. Supply at end: Copper 46, Silver 34, Gold 25, Estate 7, Duchy 5, Province 0, Curse 10, Squire 9, Market Square 9, Sage 10, Feodum 8, Fortress 8, Maroon 9, Rope 8, Tools 10, Cutthroat 9, Pendant 9.

**What happened.** Peril was the key card for both players. Each trashed its Necropolis for an early Loot. P1's Fortress loop worked: 9 Perils by T9, including a three-Loot turn on T5. But one Fortress in a 15–20 card deck only showed up every other turn. P1 also spent the T4–T8 buys on Loots and support cards instead of points, and fell behind. Meanwhile P2's ordinary Big Money got fast Loots (Prize Goat from Peril, Puzzle Box and Sword from Cutthroat) and a strong Pendant (+$5 on T7, +$7 on T10). That was enough for 5 Provinces by T13, with a $19 double-Province turn on T10. P2's Cutthroat hurt P1 twice: it cut P1 to 3 cards before a Peril turn, and it took a Loot off the Gold that P1's own Market Square reaction gained (T9). Tools, Sage and Feodum were never bought, and Maroon was bought too late to be played. The kingdom played as "Peril is strong for everyone", not as a combo that only rewards the builder. The game was decided by greening speed.
