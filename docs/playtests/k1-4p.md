# k1 — 4-player playtest log

**Kingdom (k1, "Peril combo"):** Squire, Market Square, Sage, Feodum, Fortress, Maroon, Rope, Tools, Cutthroat, Pendant
**Landscape:** Peril (Event, $2: you may trash an Action card from your hand to gain a Loot)
**Setup:** Shelters (each player starts 7 Copper + Necropolis, Hovel, Overgrown Estate). 4-player piles: 12 of each Victory card (Feodum 12), 10 per Kingdom pile, Copper 32 left after the deal, Curses 30. Loot pile: 30 cards (15 Loots x2), shuffled once with seed `k1-4p-loot-1`.
**Randomness:** every shuffle ran `node games/shuffle.cjs k1-4p-P<n>-<shuffleNumber> ...` (shuffle 1 = opening deck). 29 shuffle calls in total.

## Players and plans

- **P1 Comboist:** Fortress + Peril loop: hold a Fortress in hand, use +Buys (Market Square, Squire, Rope, Loots) to Peril it repeatedly for $2 Loots; held Market Squares turn the first trash into Gold; Cutthroat to double-dip on Loot gains; Pendant for the many differently named Loots; green once ~4 Loots.
- **P2 Pragmatist:** Cutthroat-Big Money: one Rope early, 1-2 Cutthroats (attack + Loot whenever anyone gains Gold/Loot), Gold at $6-7, Province at $8, Pendant as a late $5, Duchy dancing late; spare $2 + Buy goes to Peril on Necropolis/Squire.
- **P3 Aggressor:** Cutthroat rush: up to 3 Cutthroats (hand attack + Loot whenever anyone gains a $5+ Treasure), Fortress/Necropolis/Market Square for actions, Gold then Provinces; Peril Necropolis/Squire when $2 is spare (Squire trashed -> another Cutthroat).
- **P4 Collector:** Feodum + Silver flood: Squires (gain Silver), Market Squares for +Buy, Tools to copy Silver/Gold, then pile Feodums (1 VP per 3 Silvers each) alongside Provinces/Duchies; happy with a fat deck.

## How the bookkeeping was done

A small rules engine tracked each player's draw pile, hand, discard, play area, Durations and the trash, plus Supply and Loot pile counts. Card text was applied as written: Cutthroat stays in play until anyone next gains a $5+ Treasure; Fortress goes back to hand when trashed; Market Square reacts to your own trashes; Hovel is trashed when its owner buys a Victory card; Overgrown Estate draws when trashed; Doubloons gains a Gold; Rope, Figurehead and Jewels resolve at the start of the next turn; Endless Chalice stays in play. No Shield was in anyone's hand during an attack.
Simplified choices (all legal plays): Amphora is always used immediately, Orb always takes +1 Buy +$3, Puzzle Box never sets a card aside, and Sextant discards junk and puts the rest back best-first.

Format: `T<turn> P<n>: hand [start-of-turn 5 cards] | played [...] | $total buys n | bought/gained [...] | VP now | notes`. A "[dur]" entry is a start-of-turn Duration effect. "[since last turn]" notes are things that happened to the player on other players' turns.

### Observations for kingdom calibration

- The Peril/Fortress loop the static analysis advertised was only lukewarm in practice. P1 Perilled a Fortress just 6 times (T4, T5, T9 x2, T10 x2). It never got more than 2 Perils in a turn because +Buy was thin (one Market Square, one Squire, and Loots with +Buy). Market Square's Gold reaction **never fired**: P1 never had a held Fortress and a Market Square in hand on the same turn. P1 still won, mainly on Loot money (7 Loots, including Endless Chalice and Doubloons, which gained a Gold) turned into 5 Provinces. In effect this was "Loot money", not an engine.
- Cutthroat was the main source of interaction. It was played 5 times, each time cutting three opponents to 3 cards, and it paid out 4 Loots. 2 of those came from an opponent's gain: P3 got one off P1's Gold, and P2 got one off P1's Peril Loot. Opponents being cut to 3 drove several turns (T8 P2: $2 and nothing bought; T11 P3: $1). P1's Sword added one more attack at the end.
- Peril on Necropolis was an attractive opening play: all 4 players did it by turn 3.
- Feodum (P4) reached 4 Feodums worth 2 VP each with 6 Silvers. That was competitive (it tied for 2nd) but slow.
- Sage, Maroon, Tools and Pendant were never bought, so there was little variety. No turn crossed the "big turn" thresholds (2+ Provinces, +9 VP, 3+ Loots or $16+). The best turns were P1 T13 ($12, Province + Fortress) and P3 T15 ($11, Province + Estate).
- The game was fast and close among P1, P2 and P4 until P1 took the last two Provinces (T15, T16).
