# Dominion playtest — kingdom k2 "Repetition (2nd)", 4 players

**Kingdom:** Festival, Harbinger, Militia, Workshop, Caravan, Explorer, Outpost, Pearl Diver, Pirate Ship, Treasury (no landscapes, no extra setup).
**Supply (4p):** 12 Estate / 12 Duchy / 12 Province, 30 Curse, 10 per Kingdom pile, 32 Copper, 40 Silver, 30 Gold. Each player starts with 7 Copper + 3 Estate.
**Randomness:** every shuffle ran `shuffle.cjs` with seed `k2-4p-P<n>-<shuffleNumber>` (P1..P4, numbered from 1 = opening shuffle) on the discard pile in its current order; the printed order was used, top first. Bookkeeping (deck/hand/discard/play/duration zones, Pirate Ship tokens, trash) was done mechanically by a rules script so it could not drift.
**End condition:** Provinces gone, 3 piles empty, or 25 turns per player.

## Players and plans

- **P1 Comboist** — the Treasury + Outpost + Caravan combo: open Caravan/Silver, first $5+ is Outpost, then Treasuries (top-deck themselves into the 3-card Outpost hand) and up to 3 Caravans (their next-turn card arrives on the extra turn). Gold at $6-7, Province at $8. Plays Outpost as its terminal unless a +$2 terminal is what reaches $8.
- **P2 Pragmatist** — Militia Big Money: one Militia early, a second once the deck reaches 16 cards, Silver/Gold/Province, Duchy dancing late.
- **P3 Aggressor** — Pirate Ship ×2 + Militia, Festivals to play them together. Pirate Ship attacks until it has 4 coin tokens, then plays it for +$4.
- **P4 Collector** — Workshops (gaining Caravan/Militia/Silver, then Estates late), Festivals for +Buy, Pearl Divers with spare $2, a fat deck.

Shared endgame rules: Duchy at $5-7 once Provinces ≤ 4 (at $5 from ≤ 5), Estate at $2-4 once Provinces ≤ 2, never buy the last Province if it loses (penultimate-Province rule). Militia victims discard Victory cards, then Coppers, then the weakest card. Treasury is always top-decked when no Victory card was bought. `T<n>+` marks an Outpost extra turn. "hand" is the hand at the start of the turn after Militia discards and Caravan duration draws; cards drawn during the turn appear in brackets after the action that drew them.

Trash: 3 Gold, 4 Silver, 4 Copper (all from Pirate Ship).

**How it played out**

- The listed synergy really worked: P1 got 6 Outpost extra turns from a _single_ Outpost and 5 Treasury top-decks. Provinces came on T9, T10, T12, T15, T17+ (extra turn), T18 and T21. Caravans bought in the Outpost turn paid out at the start of the extra turn every time (T13→T13+, T15→T15+, T17→T17+).
- The combo was also streaky. Outpost's 3-card extra turns were dead or near-dead 3 times (T11+ $1, T15+ $2, T16 $0 after a Militia). Of P1's 4 dead turns, 3 came from greening plus Outpost/Militia small hands.
- Pirate Ship landed hard early. Between T5 and T10, P3 trashed 11 treasures, including Golds from P2 (twice) and P4. That crippled P2's Big Money: P2 had $8 only twice all game. From T11 onward P3 played Pirate Ship for +$4 and turned a slow start (3 VP until T15) into 2 Provinces.
- Militia was everywhere: 3 players owned one and there were 10 Militia plays, 37 discard hits and 4 blanks against 3-card hands. P1 often discarded green cards it had just bought, so the Militias hurt P1 least.
- Nobody bought Harbinger or Explorer. Workshop only fed P4's Silver/Duchy-Estate pile, which made a fat but slow deck that finished last.
