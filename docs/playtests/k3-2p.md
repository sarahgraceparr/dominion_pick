# Dominion playtest k3 (2 players)

**Kingdom (Size Distortion, 2nd ed.):** Artisan, Bandit, Bureaucrat, Chapel, Festival, Gardens, Sentry, Throne Room, Witch, Workshop. No landscapes.

**Setup:** 2 players, 7 Copper + 3 Estate each. Victory piles 8 (Gardens 8), Kingdom piles 10, Curses 10, Copper 46 after starting decks.

**Players:**

- P1 "Collector": Workshop/Gardens. Open Workshop/Workshop, Workshops gain Workshops until ~5-6, then gain/buy Gardens, then Estates; leftover buys on Copper; Festival at $5 (never reached). Pushes a 3-pile ending when ahead.
- P2 "Pragmatist": Witch + Big Money. Opened Silver/Silver on 3/4, 2 Witches, Gold at $6-7, Province at $8, Duchy/Estate in the endgame.

**Shuffles:** every shuffle used `node shuffle.cjs k3-2p-P<n>-<k> <cards>` (cards passed in alphabetical order, printed order taken top-first). P1 shuffled 6 times, P2 7 times. The shuffle note on a line means it happened during that turn (usually in the cleanup draw).

**Line format:** `T<turn> P<n>: hand (5 cards at start of turn) | played | $ total, buys | bought/gained (X(Workshop) = gained by Workshop) | VP now (opponent VP) | notes`. A dead turn means nothing other than Copper or Curse was gained.

## Result

- **Winner: P1 Collector, 41 VP to 27.**
- Game length: P1 took 18 turns, P2 took 17. The game ended on P1 turn 18 when Estates, Gardens and Workshop were all empty.
- Supply at end: Province 4, Duchy 8, Curse 8, Witch 8; Gold 24 and Silver 35 left.
- P1 deck (45 cards): {"Gardens":8,"Copper":14,"Estate":11,"Workshop":10,"Curse":2}. 8 Gardens at 4 VP each = 32, 11 Estates = 11, 2 Curses = -2, total 41.
- P2 deck (27 cards): {"Gold":6,"Province":4,"Copper":7,"Estate":3,"Witch":2,"Silver":5}. 4 Provinces = 24, 3 Estates = 3, total 27.

### What happened

- P1 had all 6 Workshops by turn 4 (2 bought on turns 1-2, then buy + Workshop gain on turns 3 and 4). P1 started gaining Gardens on turn 5 and took all 8 by turn 12; P2 never contested them. P1 then took all 8 remaining Estates by turn 16.
- P2 opened Silver/Silver and did not hit $5 until turn 6, so the first Witch arrived late. The Witch landed only twice (turns 9 and 15). On turn 15 one Witch drew the other, so the second Witch was dead.
- With Gardens and Estates gone and a lead of about 20, P1 used Workshops to gain the last 2 Workshops on turns 17-18. That emptied the third pile while P2 still had 4 Provinces left to buy. P2 managed 4 Provinces in turns 9-17, including 3 in a row on turns 15-17.
- P1 never reached $5 and never played more than one action in a turn: no Festival or Throne Room was ever bought. P1 mostly drew several Workshops at once and could only play one. It was a grindy turn-by-turn race with a low ceiling, which the Witch side could not match.
- Kingdom cards never bought: Artisan, Bandit, Bureaucrat, Chapel, Festival, Sentry, Throne Room. The Gardens rush decided the game before any engine could get going.
